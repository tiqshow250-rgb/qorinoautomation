Who We Are

Qorino Automation — a young company with a serious mission.

We are a newly founded automation startup. Our focus right now is clear and honest: we build AI software programs that automate homes, offices and shops. As we grow, we expand into smart machinery, robotics and full turnkey installations.

Problems & Solutions

Automation has real problems. We solve them.

We studied what actually goes wrong with automation projects — and built Qorino around fixing exactly those things.

01

❌ Problem

Automation systems are expensive and mostly built for large enterprises. Small homes and shops can't afford them.

✅ Qorino Solution

Software-first approach and modular packages. You start with one room or one counter, pay in stages, and expand only when you see results.

02

❌ Problem

Devices from different brands don't talk to each other. You end up with five apps and no control.

✅ Qorino Solution

Our Qorino AI layer sits on top and bridges protocols (Wi-Fi, Zigbee, MQTT, IR, Modbus) into one dashboard and one app.

03

❌ Problem

The installer disappears after the invoice. When something breaks, nobody answers.

✅ Qorino Solution

Technical support is a core product, not an afterthought. Remote diagnostics, remote fixes, on-site visits and a 24/7 helpline.

04

❌ Problem

Systems are too complicated. Staff and family members refuse to use them.

✅ Qorino Solution

Simple UI, plain-language dashboards, and hands-on training for your family, staff or shop team. Automation should feel invisible.

05

❌ Problem

Security and privacy concerns — smart cameras and locks sending data to unknown servers.

✅ Qorino Solution

Local-first processing where possible, end-to-end encryption, and full data-ownership documentation. Your data stays yours.

06

❌ Problem

Power outages and weak internet break the whole system.

✅ Qorino Solution

Offline-capable local controllers, UPS-backed hubs, and automatic re-sync when the connection returns. The system keeps working.
